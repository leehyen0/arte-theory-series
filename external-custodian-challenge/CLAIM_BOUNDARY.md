# Claim boundary

This public mirror enables recruitment and cryptographic verification. It does not itself establish:

- the real-world identity of a registrant;
- affiliation or organizational independence;
- absence of off-channel coordination;
- independent model evaluation;
- external repository repair success;
- model-weight learning;
- AGI, ASI, consciousness or sentience.

A valid registration is only `ACCEPTED_CLAIM_IDENTITY_UNVERIFIED`.

A cryptographically valid two-role quorum proves that two distinct registration objects participated in the same commit–reveal sequence. It does not automatically prove they are two independent humans.

Classification receives zero open-world transfer credit. External success requires a separately typed specialist to generate a reversible patch and pass the original repository's tests.
